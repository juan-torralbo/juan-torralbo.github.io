How I think

Principles behind every project

01

Diagnose before scaling

Most growth problems are system problems. I map where value leaks before spending another dollar on traffic.

02

One pipeline, one truth

Marketing, sales and finance work from the same stages, definitions and data, or the forecast becomes fiction.

03

Measure everything

If a stage isn’t measured, it can’t be improved. Tracking and attribution are part of the design, not a patch.

04

Automate the repetitive

Routing, reminders and reporting go to machines, so people can focus on the decisions that close deals.
